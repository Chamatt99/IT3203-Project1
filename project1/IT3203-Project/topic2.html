Cybersecurity in the Cloud

Cybersecurity includes the policies, tools, and practices used to protect systems, networks, and information. In cloud environments, customers and providers often share security responsibilities.

Common Risks

  • Stolen passwords or credentials
  • Misconfigured storage permissions
  • Phishing and social engineering
  • Unpatched applications

Protective Practices

  1. Enable multifactor authentication.
  2. Grant only the permissions users need.
  3. Keep systems and applications updated.
  4. Encrypt sensitive information.
  5. Monitor activity and maintain backups.

Shared Responsibility

The cloud provider protects parts of the underlying cloud infrastructure. The customer remains responsible for duties such as managing users, securing application settings, and protecting data, depending on the service used.
